MyWay

Turn-by-turn navigation that always takes the fastest route.

Getting started

  • Search for a place or tap a favorite, then tap GO on the route.
  • Save Home and Work from the favorites row, or with ••• on any place card.
  • Tap ☆ on a place to keep it in your favorites.
  • For a natural-sounding voice, download a Premium voice in the Settings app: Accessibility › Read & Speak › Voices › English.

Siri

  • "Hey Siri, add a stop in MyWay"
  • "Hey Siri, take me home with MyWay" / "take me to work with MyWay"
  • "Hey Siri, navigate with MyWay"
  • "Hey Siri, when will I get there in MyWay"
  • "Hey Siri, end route in MyWay"
