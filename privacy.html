Privacy Policy

MyWay · Last updated November 8, 2026

MyWay does not collect, store or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking or analytics.

Your location

MyWay uses your location only on your iPhone, to show you on the map and give turn-by-turn directions. It keeps working in the background while you navigate. Your location is never sent to us.

Services the app talks to

To plan routes and draw the map, the app sends the minimum information needed directly to these services:

  • Apple Maps (MapKit): your starting point and destinations, to calculate routes with traffic and to search for places. Covered by Apple's privacy policy.
  • OpenFreeMap: requests for map images of the area you're viewing.
  • OpenStreetMap (Overpass API): the map area around your route, to find traffic lights, stop signs and speed limits.
  • TomTom (when traffic alerts are on): the map area around you, to show crashes and road closures.

None of these requests include your name, contact details or any account information.

Data saved on your iPhone

Your favorites, Home and Work, recent searches, settings, and how long your past drives took (used to predict travel times better) are saved only on your iPhone. They are deleted when you delete the app.

Children

MyWay is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.
